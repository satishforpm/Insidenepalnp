About Inside Nepal

Inside Nepal is a static, database-free guide to Nepal's destinations — built from JSON files (places, provinces, categories) with client-side search, filtering, favorites and dark mode. Photographs are fetched live at view-time from Wikipedia's public REST API for each destination's own article, so the image shown is a genuine photograph of that specific place, credited to Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons.

Historical dates are only shown when reliably documented; where a precise founding date isn't established, that is stated plainly rather than guessed.

Contact

This is a self-contained demo build. For a production deployment, connect a real contact form endpoint (e.g. Netlify Forms) — no backend required.

Privacy & Terms

No accounts, no tracking, no server-side database. Favorites, recently-viewed and dark-mode preference are stored only in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device.
